Lab Mission

Supercomputing System and AI Lab (SSAIL) is an active research lab at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. The lab mission is (A) building the fastest and most efficient AI systems; (B) enabling new system and modeling capabilities via deep innovations, advancing the state-of-the-arts, and (C) making the technology available to everyone.
