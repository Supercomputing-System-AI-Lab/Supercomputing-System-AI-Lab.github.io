Lab Mission

Supercomputing System and AI Lab (SSAIL) is an active research lab at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. The lab mission is (A) building next-generation AI systems for scalable and efficient intelligence; (B) enabling new system and modeling capabilities via deep innovations, advancing the state-of-the-arts; and (C) making the technology available to everyone.
